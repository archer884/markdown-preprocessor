Prince Oktai was tall for a Hun—nearly six feet. In his shadow, Auxept Juniel looked tiny, and yet he managed to appear in awe at her presence. Oktai  was like that: he had a way of making anyone feel important, simply by dint of noticing them, or so it seemed. He was a man of deep and genuine humors, with little of the evasive guile that, in Aria's experience, often characterized the powerful. She could almost say she liked him. Almost.



At least he treated Juniel with respect. Then again, Juniel was the eldest daughter of one of the most prominent families of the Atlantic Empire; her father was a viscount, her mother was second or third cousin to Queen Eona herself, and Juniel was more than capable of navigating the halls of power in her own right. At twenty-six years old, she probably ought to have been married to a duke or an earl by now, ruling her own household and half the kingdom with her iron fist concealed inside a velvet glove. Instead, she had devoted her life to serving the Order. Unlike her, Aria could make no such claim.

"Sit *down!*" Oktai did not raise his voice, and somehow that was worse. "Do you *understand?*" Juniel asked. *Think*, Aria told herself. Keep your **head**. Just _breathe_.
